4 cards per carousel: 1 cover · 2 incision & recovery · 3 after-care tips · 4 doctor & booking.
Rules: no prices, no "free", no guaranteed results, no before/after promises, recovery = typical ranges.

> ⚠️ Gynecomastia is a men's surgery. The Plastic ad set targets women only, so it would not reach the right people. Recommendation: put it in `TL | LEAD | Men | M22-50`.
