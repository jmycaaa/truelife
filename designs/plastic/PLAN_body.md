## Production per carousel
1. Content (Arabic) → saved as content.md in the surgery folder
2. 2 cover model options generated with Higgsfield → best one picked, background removed
3. Body illustration with the scar line drawn (reuse the same torso illustration and draw the incision line in SVG)
4. Render 4 slides → preview → zip

## Notes for ads
- BBL and thigh lift fall under Meta's sensitive body-image rules: no close-ups of the buttocks or groin; use illustrations for the scars.
- No "before/after" claims on the slides; only the doctor's real cases with written consent.
